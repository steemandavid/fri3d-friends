# UI Ideas — graphical redesign backlog (2026-08-10)

**Status: IDEAS ONLY — nothing here is planned or started.** Rated by David on
2026-08-10; parked for after camp. Pick up from the priority order in §2.

Companion mockup sheet (11 badge-sized 296×240 mockups, one per idea):
<https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f0857d7f-ea87-4822-87a1-c48b78e5cd8f>

## 1. Why

The app's information architecture is finished and works; its *form* is not.
Everything on screen is a label. Colour appears in exactly one place (the group
pills), nothing moves except the name marquee, and Gotcha — the most dramatic
thing the badge does — is two lines of 12 px text plus five 3-pixel bars in the
bottom-right corner.

Constraints any of this must respect (all from `DESIGN.md`):

- GC9307 **296×240**, LVGL v9 on MicroPython.
- **Widgets are pre-built and hidden/shown, never created or deleted live** —
  deleting live widgets crashes this build (§8.4).
- **No backlight control** on the 2024 badge (no GPIO behind it) — a "dim"
  screensaver can only darken pixels, not the lamp.
- **The redraw budget is the binding constraint.** A `transform_scale`d
  scrolling label was already enough to starve button polling and lock the USB
  REPL. Continuous animation is unproven on this hardware.
- **No LED animation during a duel's GATT hold** — `lights.write` disables IRQs
  and would starve the link (§5.3/§5.5, already enforced in `_render_duel`).
- Everything below is drawn from data the app already has: no new hardware, no
  network, no bundled assets.

every screen is *reached*, so it lands either first or never; bolting it on

unmeasured. Before committing to the HUD, spike frame cost on-badge for

running, and confirm the arc can be redrawn at the hunt ping rate without
missing input. If it can't, #8 degrades to a static arc that only redraws on
band change (the 5-band logic in `_set_gotcha_bars` already exists).

## 3. The ideas

### 8 — Gotcha full-screen hunt HUD  ★★★★★

Promote the hunt out of the bottom gutter onto its own screen. A large `lv.arc`
proximity ring that fills as you close in, colour walking blue → amber → red on
the bands `_set_gotcha_bars` already computes; target name and emblem in the
centre; ping rate driving a pulsing halo; a red vignette + shake when *you* are
being hunted; the respawn countdown as a draining ring. Replaces `_g_chip`,
`_g_target` and the five `_g_bars`.

Must keep the existing "one LED write, held, no animation during the hold" rule
and the A-action labelling from `reveal_action_label()`.

### 9 — Paged carousel navigation  ★★★★★

Joystick left/right pages between **Nametag · Radar/Vrienden · Gotcha ·
Instellingen** with a slide transition and a dot indicator, replacing today's
stack of hidden overlays (`_detail_panel`, `_menu`, `_setup_overlay`,
`_adopt_panel`, `_consent`).

The hard part is the LVGL focus group: `_apply_focus` / `_set_focus` currently
reconcile membership per *state*; they'd need to reconcile per *page*, with the
Menu button no longer the universal default focus. The drawer-pop bug (empty
default group → keypad falls through to the OS top bar) is the thing to not
regress.

### 3 — Icon status ribbon  ★★★★

One slim strip at the top replacing the bare `14:22` / `87%` labels: clock, a
drawn battery cell that fills and colours by level, a BLE dot that pulses on
every advert actually received (proof-of-life that is currently invisible), a
friend-count chip, and the Gotcha state chip promoted out of the bottom gutter.
Frees the bottom of the screen for #8/#9. Cheapest visible win on the list.

### 7 — Toast notification system  ★★★★

Replaces `_show_banner` / `_coalesced_text`, which flattens every event into one
centred string. Toasts slide down, stack, carry an icon and a type colour
(arrival · contact swap · revealed · under attack), and show a hairline that
drains as the dismiss timer runs. Critical states (under attack, dead) stay
pinned until the state clears — same semantics as today's `persistent` flag,
different presentation. Makes it possible to see what happened while you weren't
looking.

### 10 — Motion pack  ★★★★

Three small things under one budget, to land *after* whatever else ships:

- **Splash that assembles itself** — logo rings out, name types in, LED sweep in
  sync with the type-on. (`_build_splash` / `_splash_then_enter` today just
  shows a static frame for a fixed delay.)
- **Micro-animations on state change** — a pill sliding in on group adopt,
  counters counting up, a blip popping on arrival.
- **Idle screensaver** — after N minutes, dim to a slowly drifting name plus a
  starfield; wake on any button or any arrival. Note: pixels only, the backlight
  cannot be dimmed on the 2024 badge, so this is cosmetic, not a power saving.

### 11 — Camp history page  ★ (liked, unrated)

A page you show people on the last night: a sparkline of friends-nearby across
the day, the five people you spent the most time near, and a running count of
unique badges met. Needs a small rolling on-device log (bucketed counts, not raw
sightings) — which is new state, so it must go through `state_backup.py` to
survive an AppStore update.

### 1 — Palette engine  ★★

Derive the whole theme from your primary group's hue instead of the hardcoded
`COL_BG` / `COL_NEAR` / `COL_HINT` constants: gradient ground, accent borders,
focus rings, and the idle LED breathe all in one family. Low rating, but it is
the substrate the others sit on — reconsider if several of the visual ideas
land together.

### 4 — Radar view  ★★

Friends as blips on a polar radar: rings are RSSI bands, blip colour is the
group, blip size is signal, and the **angle is a stable hash of the badge ID**
so people keep their position instead of jittering. A slow sweep line; A opens
the existing detail list.

### 5 — Orbit constellation  ★★

Alternative to #4, not an addition. Your name stays centred and large; friends
drift on slow orbits at radii set by signal, with faint lines joining same-group
members. Arrivals fly in from the edge, departures fade.

### 6 — Deterministic emblems  ★★

Hash the badge ID into 4–6 coloured shapes on a fixed grid — a little
generated coat of arms, shown beside names in the detail list, in arrival
toasts, on Gotcha targets, and large on your own nametag. Zero assets, zero
network, stable per badge.

### 2 — Festival-pass nametag  ★

Re-cut the top half as a laminated event pass: tinted header band with the camp
wordmark and badge number, name on its own inset plate, group pills as pass
privileges, a lanyard notch. Rated lowest — the nametag reads fine as-is.
